TacoFile Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-01

TacoFile (the "app") is a Windows program by TacoScore that turns Piascore scores and GoodNotes notebooks on an iPad or iPhone into TacoScore files. This policy explains what information the app handles.


1. Personal information we collect

The app does not collect personal information and does not send anything to any server. There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics or usage reporting.


2. What the app reads on your PC

When you click its button, the app reads the following from your own device connected by USB cable. All of it is processed only on your PC.

- The device's name and identifier (UDID), and the PC's pairing record for it: to recognize the connected device.
- A device backup: to get the Piascore and GoodNotes files, the app makes a backup of the device in a temporary folder on your PC. The backup may contain other personal data from the device, but the app takes out only the Piascore and GoodNotes files, and deletes the backup as soon as the work finishes or is cancelled.
- Piascore scores and GoodNotes notebooks and handwriting: to turn them into TacoScore (.tco) files.


3. What the app stores on your PC

- The Piascore and GoodNotes files it took out, and the TacoScore files it made: in the folder you choose (by default Documents\TacoFile).
- Settings (output folder, window size): %APPDATA%\TacoFile\settings.json

These files stay on your PC and are yours to manage.


4. Sharing with third parties

The app does not share or send information to anyone, including TacoScore.

- "Send to Device" copies the files you select over the USB cable only to the TacoScore app on your own device.
- The app uses Apple's "Apple Devices" software to talk to the device. If it isn't installed, the app can install it from the Microsoft Store; Microsoft's and Apple's privacy policies apply to that. The app sends none of your information in doing so.


5. Your controls and how to delete

Everything is on your PC, so you can see and delete it at any time:

- Select files in the app and click Delete, or delete the files in the output folder (by default Documents\TacoFile).
- Uninstall the app and delete the %APPDATA%\TacoFile folder to remove the settings.


6. Children

The app collects no one's personal information, including children's.


7. Changes

If the app's features change and this policy is updated, the new text and its effective date will be posted on this page.
