TacoScore (hereinafter the "App") considers the protection of Users' personal information important and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws.

## Article 1 (Principles of Personal Information Processing)
① Through the "Sign in with Apple" feature, the Company collects a User's Apple account identifier, email address (if the User consents to sharing it), and name (if the User consents to sharing it), and stores this information in a database managed by the Company via CloudKit, a cloud service provided by Apple.
② Sheet music, annotations, memos, and other User Content that a User creates or edits within the App is stored on the User's device and is not uploaded to or stored on the Company's servers.
③ Some usage information generated in the course of using the App may be transmitted to and processed on Apple's servers through platform or analytics features provided by Apple, for purposes such as improving the App's features and analyzing usage patterns.

## Article 2 (Information Processed)
1. Information collected through Sign in with Apple: Apple account unique identifier, email address (optional), name (optional)
2. Service usage and device-related information: device model, operating system version, app version, registration date, last active date, number of documents held
3. Subscription-related information: subscription status and expiration date (processed through the App Store; the Company does not itself retain payment information)
4. The following usage information generated in the course of using the App:
   - Menus selected by the User, the sequence and path of navigation between menus
   - Features used and frequency of use
   - Titles of music created or selected within the App
   - Information related to the App's launch, errors, or abnormal behavior
   - Other usage information necessary for the App's stability and feature improvement
If a User uses the App without registering (i.e., without signing in via Sign in with Apple), the information described in Items 1 and 3 is not collected.

## Article 3 (Purposes of Processing Personal Information and Usage Information)
Information processed within the App may be used for the following purposes.
1. Member identification and management of subscription status
2. Improving the App's features, stability, and performance
3. Analyzing App usage patterns and feature-usage trends
4. Identifying errors and abnormal behavior
5. Improving user experience and developing new features or improving existing ones
